# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

Contributors are expected to keep the project welcoming, respectful, and professional.

## Expected Behavior

- Use respectful and inclusive language.
- Provide constructive feedback.
- Focus on the technical work and project goals.
- Be patient with questions and reviews.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Disruptive or intentionally harmful contributions.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, issues, pull requests, or contributions that do not meet these standards.
